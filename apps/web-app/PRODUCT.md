# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Leonly is for two people in a romantic relationship who want a private place to preserve and revisit
their shared history. Each person participates as a member of the same shared space.

## Product Purpose

Leonly helps a couple collect, organize, and relive meaningful memories together. Success means both
members can trust the space as a lasting, private record of their relationship rather than content
prepared for an outside audience.

## Positioning

Leonly is not a social network or a generic photo album. It is a private digital sanctuary designed
for exactly two people, organized around their shared history with no external audience.

## Operating Context

Members use Leonly through an authenticated web application on mobile and desktop. One person creates
a shared space and invites the other. Together they add dated memories, photos, locations, comments,
and reactions, then revisit them through the shared dashboard, chronological timeline, memory detail,
and Private Vault.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A person can belong to at most one active shared space, and an active space supports at most two
  active members.
- Invitations provide the controlled path for the second member to join.
- Memories can appear in the shared timeline or the shared Private Vault. The Vault is private from
  outsiders, but it does not hide content from either member.
- Memory media and unavailable resources must preserve strict authorization and non-enumerating
  privacy boundaries.
- Product screens must use persisted information and honest empty states; they must not fabricate
  memories, places, people, or proof.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Leonly name and its identity as a private, intimate product centered on the relationship
between two people.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains implemented product workflows and behavioral specifications. No testimonials,
customer logos, usage metrics, press, case studies, or external product claims are available; future
work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Protect the couple's privacy before optimizing convenience or visibility.
- Design every core experience for two equal participants in one shared history.
- Keep memories emotionally meaningful while making their organization and retrieval dependable.
- Represent persisted product truth honestly, including empty, unavailable, and waiting states.
- Make important actions and recovery paths understandable across mobile and desktop contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows must remain keyboard operable, visibly focused, semantically named, understandable
without color alone, responsive at supported viewport sizes, and compatible with reduced motion.
